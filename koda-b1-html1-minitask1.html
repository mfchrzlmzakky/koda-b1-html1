Barcelona 5th, Man Utd 9th & Galatasaray 37th: Top 100 most valuable squads in the world

Transfermarkt has released their first market value updates of the season across all the major leagues and we can now analyse which clubs possess the most valueable squads. There’s been significant changes across the top five leagues; English Premier League, LaLiga, Serie A, Ligue 1 and the Bundesliga. The valuations of thousands of players have been adjusted with Lamine Yamal and Erling Haaland remaining the most valuable players in the world at €220m.

Yan Diomande received the biggest upgrade of any player following his move to Real Madrid, while Jamal Musiala’s valuation dropped the most, with his German teammate Florian Wirtz second. With valuations locked in until the next updates in December, the top 100 clubs ranked by market value are positioned and we’ve looked closer at the top twenty, but you can view the full list below.

The top 20 most valuable squads

Real Madrid still possess the most valuable squad in the world at €1.5 billion and their overall valuation increased by €40m in the LaLiga update. Los Blancos have five players valued at €100m or higher with Kylian Mbappé their most valuable player at €200m. Manchester City ranked second, slightly lower than Madrid, despite being rounded up to €1.5b with their squad value soaring by €66m following the summer additions of Enzo Fernández, Ayyoub Bouaddi and Elliot Anderson.

As the graphic above illustrates, Arsenal rank third at €1.38b with their valuation increasingly slightly by €48m. Mikel Arteta’s squad is arguably still the strongest in the Premier League and Max Dowman and Myles Lewis-Skelly received €20m upgrades. Reigning Champions League holders PSG and Barcelona complete the top five with Chelsea, Liverpool and Bayern Munich the three other clubs valued over €1b. Manchester United and Tottenham complete the top ten, with Spurs’ squad still valued highly despite their continued underachievement under Robert De Zerbi.

Unsurprisingly, the top 20 list is dominated by Premier League clubs with 12 featuring in the top ten list with only six non-English clubs more valuable than Brighton at €700m. PSG are the only Ligue 1 representative, Borussia Dortmund join Bayern from the Bundesliga and Inter Milan the most valuable Serie A club at €738m.
